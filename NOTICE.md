## What is in this repository and where it came from

**Our own work, GPL-3:** everything in `src/`, `plugin/`, `tools/` and `docs/` except the files listed
below. The engine is a clean rewrite in C++ of what the FS1R's CPU does between MIDI and the tone
generator registers, worked out by reading the v1.20 firmware. It contains no Yamaha code.

**Extracted from Yamaha's EPROM, not ours to license:**

- `src/fs1r_rom_tables.h` - the CPU-side conversion tables (velocity curves, level scaling, the note
  table, the pan tables and the rest), generated by `tools/extract_tables.py` from a v1.20 EPROM image.
- `src/fs1r_algorithms.h` - the 88 algorithm words.
- `presets/` - the 1408 preset voices, as `.syx` files and an index, generated by
  `tools/extract_presets.py`.
- `docs/vop3_microcode.md` and `docs/vop3/` - the YSS236 effect DSP's program and coefficient tables,
  generated by `tools/extract_vop3.py`. Reference material; nothing here depends on it.

These are Yamaha's data. They are included because the emulation is worthless without the exact tables,
and because the tools that regenerate them from an EPROM image are in the repository. If you object to
their presence, the generators reproduce every one of them from a dump you make yourself.

**Not in this repository:** the FS1R firmware, the EPROM image and the SH7044 flash. You need your own
dump to use `-r`, `tools/extract_*.py` or the plugin's EPROM button.

**Documentation quoted from:** the FS1R owner's manual and Data List (Yamaha, 1998) and US patent
5,610,354. The text files under `docs/` are conversions kept for reference while working; the parameter
tables in `plugin/parameterDescriptions_fs1r.json` are derived from them.

**The panel artwork** in `plugin/fs1r_panel.svg` is the front panel figure from page 14 of the FS1R
owner's manual (Yamaha, 1998), converted to vectors. It is Yamaha's drawing, redistributed here for
the same reason as the parameter tables: the plugin is a controller for a specific piece of hardware
and is not useful if it does not look like one. Not ours to license.

**Third party, under their own licences:** JUCE (`extern/JUCE`, dual AGPL/commercial) and
clap-juce-extensions with the CLAP SDK (`extern/clap-juce-extensions`, MIT). Neither is redistributed
here; both are submodules.
